# Reporting audit, 2026-09-24

Scope: everything under `/admin/reporting` (Overview, P&L, Sales & Products, Costs, Marketing, Capacity, Projections) and the data behind it: the ledger, the Square and Stripe settlement jobs, the Plaid bank feed, and the categorization rules.

Method: every ledger table was dumped and checked for internal consistency (balanced entries, orphans, duplicates, settlement rows vs. their journal entries). Then each source was re-pulled from its API and compared to the ledger day by day: Square (Orders, Payments, Refunds, Gift Card Activities, Payouts), Stripe (balance transactions since Jan 2025), Plaid (every transaction the three live connections still return, plus live balances). A separate read of every report view and route looked for aggregation and date bugs.

## Verified correct

- Every one of the 1,467 journal entries balances; no orphan lines; every revenue settlement has exactly one journal entry and vice versa; no bank transaction has more than one entry.
- Square: all 369 stored settlement days match a fresh recomputation from the Square API to the cent, on every field (net sales, tax, gift cards, fees, cash). No missing days apart from a pre-opening test day (2025-07-19, net -$2.83) and yesterday, which posts tonight.
- The eight days where the settlement row and its journal entry disagree are the gift-card comp days; the manual correction entries bring the ledger to the same figure. No action needed.
- Bank feed: the old (removed) Plaid connections hold June 2025 to June 2 2026 and the live ones hold June 3 2026 onward. They do not overlap, so nothing is double-imported across connections. Every transaction Plaid still returns for the live connections is in the database.
- Square payouts to Chase reconcile with the ledger's clearing transfers (about $117k each) apart from the four payouts still in transit. Stripe payouts reconcile exactly once in-transit payouts are counted.

## Fixed today (data)

1. **Stripe revenue was missing for 66 days.** Online bookings launched 2026-03-24 but Stripe settlements only started 2026-06-04. Days from Aug 2025 (early test-mode bookings with real payouts) and Mar 24 to Jun 3 2026 never posted. This is why the Stripe clearing account sat at -$3,093. Posted through the app's own idempotent job: $3,464 net revenue, $130 fees. The P&L now shows Stripe revenue in March, April and May 2026 that it did not before.
2. **Square Capital repayments were never recorded.** Square has withheld a slice of every payout since the $11,400 advance in June 2026. The daily settlement job cannot see this (it is only in the Payouts API), so the Square Clearing account grew by about $1,300 a month while the liability stayed at $11,400. Posted 112 per-payout entries: $4,987 of principal repaid and $139 of gift-card-load and instant-deposit fees. Square Clearing went from $7,287 to $2,161 (the remainder is cash sales plus payouts in transit). The nightly cron now does this automatically.
3. **One duplicated Amazon charge ($46.16).** A pending Amex transaction was categorized by hand on Sep 10, then Plaid replaced it with the settled version, which the rules posted again. The sync tried to delete the first entry but a foreign key made the delete fail silently. The duplicate entry is removed and the sync is fixed.

## Fixed today (code, deployed)

- P&L and Costs: a custom range starting mid-month dropped that whole month (the month filter compares against the 1st). Sales and Marketing kept it, so the tabs disagreed.

"Net Sales" is labeled Square-only and the "since grand opening" caption only shows for the ALL range.

- Booking charts bucketed by UTC day in some places and browser-local in others, so evening bookings landed on different days on the same tab. All use the studio's calendar day now.
- Revenue drill-downs showed negative amounts under a positive P&L cell (raw ledger sign). Flipped for revenue accounts.
- Journal drill-down fetched the 2,000 newest entries and filtered in JS, which would silently drop older matches once the ledger grew past that (it is at 1,644 now). It filters on the server.
- Seasonality callout picked the in-progress month by UTC month.
