*By Sam, Owner of Scorched Studio*

Saturday night at Scorched is a party. Every seat is taken, three playlists are fighting for the speaker, and somebody is always burning a surprisingly good portrait of their dog.

Monday and Tuesday are a different studio. Same tools, same wall of traceable designs, same fridge full of drinks. The difference is that you can pick any seat in the room, take your time, and our team is right there the moment you want a second opinion on your shading.

We would like more of you to see that version of the place. So for all of November, Mondays and Tuesdays are **Weeknight Burn**.

## The offer

- **Every Monday and Tuesday in November**, from November 2 through November 30, 5 to 10 PM.
- **Come with someone and the second $15 entry is free.** Two people, one entry fee, plus whatever projects you choose. Bring four and two entries are free.
- **To claim it**, book at [scorchedstudio.com/book](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/book) or walk in, then say **WEEKNIGHT** at the counter.

Projects start at $5 for a ring and $6 for a wood disc. A cutting board is $14. Everything else is included: the burner, the designs, and the help.

## Three reasons a weeknight burn is the better burn

1. **You get the room.** Spread out, bring the friend who always says they are "not creative," and give them the seat by the window. They will be fine. The traceable designs are undefeated.

2. **You get us.** On a packed Saturday our team is moving fast. On a Tuesday we can sit down next to you, show you how to get a clean line on a curve, and talk you out of rushing the final pass.

3. **You get a head start on gifts.** A cutting board or coaster set burned by you beats anything you can order, and November is the month to make them before the December rush. Burn two on a Monday and you have crossed two names off the list for less than dinner.

## Make it a plan

Pick a Monday or Tuesday, text the person you keep meaning to see, and [book your seats](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/book). If you would rather not plan, just show up after work. We will have a seat warm for you.

See you on a weeknight.
