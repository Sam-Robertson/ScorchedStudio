Looking for something new? Something hot? Welcome to Scorched Wood Burning Studio.

*(Oh, what's that? Another date night spot in Utah county? Yes. But we've got drinks and good music and your date isn't sick of this venue. A rarity!)*

In the sweet spot between kindling and conversation starter, wood burning is a creative outlet for those looking for a new craft. Using heat pens that burn at a scorching 700° Fahrenheit, you can turn your average cutting board into an art piece. Hot girl summer who?

After signing a waiver (what's fun without a little danger?) choose between several products, like a coaster set, leather bracelet, jewelry box, wallet, and more. If you're looking for a structure, we have stencils to choose from. Or, if you're feeling brave, burn your own design.

And, lucky you! We've committed to August 1st as our Grand Opening! *(We are still very much in renovation, but we're hustling. Please appreciate our efforts.)*

You should come! Free drinks. Free wood burning. Free date night. Free lunch, if you're a beaver. Free social outing. Free burning, scorching, pyromaniac fun.

Bring your mom. Bring your crush. Bring your friend-but-I-wish-we-were-more. *(Trust us on this one.)* We'd love to have you! And your apartment could use some fresh art on the wall.

See you there?
