*By Sam, Owner of Scorched Studio*

Most gifts are forgotten by February. A handmade one is not.

When you burn a cutting board for your mom, she is not just getting a cutting board. She is getting an evening you spent thinking about her, the design you picked because it reminded you of her kitchen, and your handwriting in the wood. It costs less than most things you would buy her, and it is the one she keeps.

Here is how to make your gifts at Scorched this year, with prices, so you can plan the evening.

## What people actually make

Entry is $15, and that includes the burner, our wall of traceable designs, a drink, a Polaroid and the wax finish. The project is extra, and the prices are small enough that most people leave with more than one.

- **Wood disc ornament, $6.** A slice of pine with the bark still on. Burn a name and the year, or the family dog, and it goes on the tree every December for the rest of time. This is the cheapest gift in the building and the one people cry over.
- **Cutting board, $14.** The classic. A last name across the handle, a favorite recipe in your own handwriting, or the outline of the state. Good for parents, in-laws, and anyone who just bought a house.
- **Coaster set, $16.** Four small canvases for an inside joke. Great for a roommate or a sibling.
- **Leather book, $14.** Burn initials or a short line on the cover. This is the one for the person who journals, or says they are going to.
- **Rings and bracelets, $5 and $7.** Stocking stuffers you can finish in twenty minutes.

Take a look at the full list on our [home page](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/#pricing) if you want to see the rest, including plates, cups, wallets and the wireless charger.

## You do not have to be artistic

We mean this. The traceable designs do the drawing for you, and the burner does the shading if you let it. Your job is to go slow and keep the tip moving. Our team walks the room the whole time, and if a line goes sideways we will show you how to turn it into something on purpose.

If you want to burn something of your own, a logo, a kid's doodle or a line drawing you found, upload it ahead of time through our [print design](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/print-design) page. We will scale it to your project and have it ready to trace when you sit down.

## Go before the rush

The second half of December is our busiest two weeks of the year, and the good seats go first. November is the month to make gifts, for two reasons.

1. **You have time to do it right.** Nobody burns their best work on December 22 with a list in their pocket.
2. **Mondays and Tuesdays are two for one this month.** Bring someone, say **WEEKNIGHT** at the counter, and the second $15 entry is free. Two people, two gifts each, and you are out for less than a dinner. The details are in the [Weeknight Burn post](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/blog/weeknight-burn).

## Give the night instead

Some people would rather make the gift themselves than receive one you made. For them, a gift card is the move. Load it with any amount, and it covers entry and projects alike.

- **Digital gift cards** are sent by email and work for last-minute gifts. [Buy one here](https://app.squareup.com/gift/ML3N1RN3EGATW/order).
- **Physical gift cards** are at the counter in Orem, if you want something to wrap.

A $30 card covers an entry and a cutting board. A $60 card sends two people on a date.

## Bring the whole office

If your team, your friend group or your church group is looking for a holiday activity that is not another dinner, we host groups of all sizes. Everyone leaves with something they made, and we handle setup and cleanup. Group entry starts at $15 per person and drops as the group grows, with projects priced separately. The rates and the request form are on our [group events](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/group-events) page. December weekends fill early, so send the request this week if you have a date in mind.

Pick a night, pick two names off your list, and [book your seats](https://www.scorchedstudio.com/book). We are at 218 E University Pkwy in Orem, and we will have the burners warm.
